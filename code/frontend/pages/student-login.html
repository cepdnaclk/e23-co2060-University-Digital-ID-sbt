Student Wallet Login

Connect your registered MetaMask wallet and prove ownership by signing a secure login message.

Secure passwordless access

PeraSoul uses MetaMask signatures instead of usernames and passwords for student authentication.

Your private key never leaves MetaMask.
The backend generates a unique nonce for every login.
MetaMask signs the login message securely.
The backend verifies that the signature belongs to your wallet.
